# Getting the survey app onto your phone

Managalas Small-Scale Mining Site & Miner Survey

You do this **once**, and you need internet **only for this setup**. After that
the app works with no signal anywhere in the field.

---

## What you need

- An Android phone with **Chrome**, or an iPhone with **Safari**
- Internet for about five minutes
- The link from your supervisor
- Free space on the phone — photographs are kept at full quality, so allow
  **about 500 MB** if you will do many surveys before sending them

---

## Method 1 — Install from the link (recommended)

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome**. Do not use Facebook's browser or any other app's browser.
2. Type in the link your supervisor sent, or tap it from the message.
3. Wait for the survey screen to appear.
4. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
5. Tap **Add to Home screen** (it may say **Install app**).
6. Tap **Install** or **Add**.

### iPhone (Safari)

1. Open **Safari**. It must be Safari — this does not work in Chrome on iPhone.
2. Type in the link your supervisor sent, or tap it from the message.
3. Wait for the survey screen to appear.
4. Tap **Share** — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom.
5. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
6. Tap **Add** at the top right.

### Then, on both

You now have an icon on your home screen. **Open the app from that icon from
now on**, not from the browser.

---

## Method 2 — Install from a file

If your supervisor sends you a file called `MCA_SAMS_Survey.html` instead of a
link:

1. Save the file to your phone. Do not rename it.
2. Open it with your browser — Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone.
3. Use it the same way.

**Important:** do not move or rename this file afterwards. If you do, the phone
may treat it as a different app and your saved surveys will not appear. Send any
completed surveys before moving anything.

The link method is safer for real fieldwork. Use the file only if you cannot get
online.

---

## Check it works before you go

Do this **while you still have signal**:

1. Turn on **flight mode**.
2. Open the app from the home screen icon.
3. It should open normally and let you start a survey.
4. Turn flight mode off.

If it opens in flight mode, you are ready. If it does not, open the link once
more with internet on, wait for the survey screen, then try again.

---

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No **Add to Home screen** option | You are not in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). Open the link in the right browser. |
| App will not open offline | Open the link once with internet, wait for the survey list, then test again. |
| "Add to Home Screen" missing on iPhone | Scroll further down the share list. It sits below the sharing apps. |
| Old version showing after an update | Close the app fully, open once with internet, and it will update itself. |
| Phone says storage full | Send your completed surveys, then delete them from the app. |

## Before your first real interview

- [ ] App icon is on the home screen
- [ ] App opens in flight mode
- [ ] You have run one practice survey all the way through
- [ ] You have taken practice photographs and seen them appear
- [ ] You have sent that practice survey to your supervisor
- [ ] Your supervisor has confirmed it arrived and the photographs are clear
- [ ] You have deleted the practice survey

Then read the **Interviewer Guide** before going out.

---

## Things that will lose your data

Read these twice. There is no backup — the surveys exist only on your phone
until you send them.

- **Do not clear browsing data, cache or site data.** This deletes every survey
  that has not been sent.
- **Do not uninstall the app** while unsent surveys are on it.
- **Do not use private / incognito mode.**
- **Send surveys regularly** — do not carry two weeks of work on one phone.
