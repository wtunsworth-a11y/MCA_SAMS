# Interviewer guide

Managalas Small-Scale Mining Site & Miner Survey

For enumerators conducting the interview. Read this before your first day in the
field. Set the app up first — see [`INSTALL_GUIDE.md`](INSTALL_GUIDE.md).

---

## What this survey is for

To understand small-scale mining in and around the Managalas Conservation Area:
how people mine, what they earn, what it does to the land and rivers, what
hazards they face, and what support would help. The findings inform planning for
safer and more sustainable mining alongside conservation.

The survey takes **30–45 minutes**, plus time for photographs.

## Your job

You are recording what the miner tells you. You are **not** inspecting,
enforcing, or advising.

- Ask the question as written. Do not reword it to what you expect.
- Record the answer given, even where you believe it is wrong.
- Do not warn anyone about mercury, safety or legality **during** the interview.
  It changes the answers to every question that follows. If someone asks you
  directly, offer to talk after the interview is finished.
- Never promise money, equipment, a licence or protection from consequences.

---

## Before you approach anyone

1. Check the app opens (flight mode test).
2. Check the phone is charged. A full survey with photographs uses real battery.
3. Know your Survey ID for the day — your supervisor sets the format, e.g.
   `MGL-2026-014`.
4. Look at the site from a distance and plan your photographs.

## Starting a survey

Tap **New survey** and enter:

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| Name of mining site | The local name for the site (this fills Q3) |
| Survey ID | The reference from your supervisor (this fills Q1) |
| Enumerator | Your own name |

Get the **Survey ID right**. It becomes the filename of everything you send and
the prefix of every photograph. A wrong ID is hard to untangle later.

---

## Section 0 — Consent. Do not skip this.

Read the consent script **aloud, in full**, in the language the person is most
comfortable in. It is on screen in Section 0.

Cover, in your own words if translating:

- Who you are and who the survey is for
- That taking part is **voluntary**
- That they may **skip any question** or **stop at any time**
- That answers are **confidential** and used for research and planning
- That it takes **30–45 minutes**

Then record the answer.

**If the person declines, stop.** Thank them and leave. Do not ask "just a few"
questions. Do not photograph the site. Record the decline and close the survey.

Take the signature or mark, and the date.

> Consent is not a form to get past. If someone seems uneasy, uncertain, or is
> agreeing only because others are watching, stop and come back another time.

### Who you may interview

- Adults only.
- The person actually mining, not a spokesperson answering on their behalf.
- One person at a time where you can manage it. If a group answers together,
  note that in the enumerator's comments.

## Working through the sections

The app shows sixteen sections. Work top to bottom. Each shows how many
questions are answered, so you can see what is left.

**Answers save as you type.** If the phone dies or you close the app, nothing is
lost. You can stop mid-interview and come back.

Questions marked with a number (1–93) match the paper form exactly. If someone
prefers paper, or the phone fails, the numbers line up.

### Question types

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A single circle list | Choose **one** answer |
| Square boxes | Choose **as many as apply** |
| **Other** | Choosing it opens a box — type what they actually said |
| A box appearing after an answer | A follow-up. It only appears when relevant |
| Several small boxes side by side | Fill the ones that apply (e.g. Men / Women / Youth / Children) |

Leave a question **blank** if the person will not answer or does not know. Do not
guess, and do not put zero — a blank and a zero mean different things.

## Notes on particular sections

### Section 2 — Land ownership (Q12–Q17)

Land is sensitive. If a dispute is live, record what you are told and move on.
Do not ask follow-up questions to settle who is right.

### Section 3 — Mining operation (Q18–Q26)

**Q21** counts people, not families. Ask about the operation, not the household.

**Q25 and Q26** pair with your Equipment photographs. Tick every item they use,
then note in Q26 which they own and which they hire — this is often the more
useful answer, and it is easy to forget to ask.

### Section 5 — Mercury (Q33–Q42)

The most sensitive part of the survey. People may fear consequences for
answering honestly.

- Ask flatly, without warning or judgement in your voice.
- Do not explain mercury's dangers here. That belongs after the interview.
- "Previously used" is a real answer — offer it where it fits.
- **Q40** — many people have never heard the word *retort*. "Don't know what a
  retort is" is a genuine, useful answer. Do not explain it and then re-ask.

### Section 6 — Safety (Q43–Q48)

If someone describes a current, serious hazard, finish the survey, then raise it
with your supervisor the same day.

### Section 7 and 8 — Environment and rehabilitation (Q49–Q61)

**Q58–Q60** — "Never" rehabilitating is a normal answer, not a failing. Record it
without comment. You still take the Rehabilitation photograph.

### Section 13 — Views on conservation (Q85–Q91)

These are opinions. There is no right answer. If someone gives a long answer,
record the sense of it in the free-text box rather than forcing a category.

### Sections 14 — Final comments (Q92–Q93)

Let them talk. These answers are read carefully — they are where people say what
the categories missed. Write down what they say, not a summary of it.

## Photographs

The app requires a minimum for each subject and will show what is still
outstanding.

| Subject | Photos | What matters |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mining Site | 4 | A wide shot showing the site in the landscape; the working face or pit; the deposit being worked; where the work meets water |
| **Equipment** | **8** | **One frame per item.** The pan, the shovel, the sluice box, the pump, each machine — separately |
| Processing Activity | 4 | The recovery process actually running; where mercury is handled; where amalgam is burned |
| Gold Recovered | 2 | The gold, with a coin or rule in frame for scale; it being weighed if possible |
| Rehabilitation | 1 | The work done — **or the untouched ground if none has been done** |

### Rules for photographs

- **Ask before photographing people.** Consent to the interview is not consent
  to be photographed. Prefer photographs of the site and equipment without
  faces.
- **Equipment needs the most shots.** One photograph of a pile of tools records
  nothing about what is present. Each item gets its own frame.
- **Rehabilitation is required even when there is none.** Photograph the bare,
  un-rehabilitated ground. That absence is the finding.
- Get close enough that any nameplate, serial number or label can be read.
- Steady the phone. A blurred photograph of a serial number is worthless.
- Photograph in daylight where you can.

Each photograph is given an ID like `MGL-2026-014-EQP-03`, shown under the
picture. Tap a photograph to see it full size and check it is sharp before you
move on. Delete and retake anything blurred — while you are still at the site.

## The enumerator's observation

The last section is **your** judgement, not the miner's. Complete it before you
leave, while you can still see the site.

- **Overall mining intensity** and **environmental concern** — your assessment
- **Site GPS** — record the position if you can get a fix, and note it
- **Photographs taken** and **Photo IDs** — the app fills these in from the
  photographs you took; you do not type them
- **Comments** — anything the questions did not capture: who else was present,
  whether answers seemed guarded, conditions on the day
- Your name, signature and the date

---

## Finishing and sending

1. Check the survey screen — every section shows its count.
2. Read the thank-you to the respondent.
3. Tap **Send survey + photos**.
4. Choose how to send it — WhatsApp, email, Google Drive, whatever works where
   you are.

If you have no signal, tap **Save to this device instead** and send it when you
next reach a connection.

One file carries the answers **and** every photograph. Do not send photographs
separately.

**Send the same day where you can.** Do not carry a week of unsent surveys.

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Phone dies mid-interview | Charge it and reopen. Answers are saved. |
| Person stops partway | Record what you have. Note why in the comments. Send it anyway. |
| Wrong Survey ID entered | Tell your supervisor the wrong and right IDs. Do not start again. |
| Photograph is blurred | Delete it in the app and retake before leaving the site. |
| No signal for days | Keep working. Surveys stay on the phone. Send when you reach signal. |
| App will not open | Do not clear browser data. Contact your supervisor — the surveys are usually still there. |

---

## Remember

- Consent first, every time.
- Record what is said, not what you expect.
- No warnings or advice during the interview.
- Equipment: one photograph per item.
- Rehabilitation: a photograph even when there is none.
- Send the same day where you can.
