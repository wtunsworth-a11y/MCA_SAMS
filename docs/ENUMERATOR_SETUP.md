# Setting up the survey app on your phone

You only need to do this once, and you need internet **only for this setup**.
After that the app works with no signal at all.

## 1. Open the link

Your supervisor will send you a web link. Open it in your phone's browser —
**Chrome** on Android, **Safari** on iPhone.

## 2. Add it to your home screen

- **Android / Chrome:** tap the **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen** → **Install**.
- **iPhone / Safari:** tap **Share** (the box with an arrow) → **Add to Home
  Screen** → **Add**.

You should now have an icon on your home screen. Open the app from that icon
from now on, not from the browser.

## 3. Check it works offline

Turn on flight mode and open the app from the icon. It should open normally. If
it does, you are ready for the field.

---

## Doing an interview

1. Tap **New survey**. Enter the mining site name, the Survey ID your supervisor
   gave you, and your own name.
2. Work through the sections in order. **Section 0 is the consent script** —
   read it aloud before asking anything else, and record the answer.
3. Answers save as you go. You can leave the app and come back; nothing is lost.
4. Take the photographs. Each subject shows how many are still needed.
5. When you are done, tap **Send survey + photos**.

## Photographs

The app tells you the minimum for each subject:

| Subject | Photos |
| --- | ---: |
| Mining Site | 4 |
| Equipment | 8 |
| Processing Activity | 4 |
| Gold Recovered | 2 |
| Rehabilitation | 1 |

**Equipment needs the most.** Photograph each item on its own — the pan, the
shovel, the sluice box, the pump, each machine. One photo of everything piled
together is not enough.

**Rehabilitation always needs a photo, even if nothing has been done.** In that
case, photograph the bare ground. That is the record.

## Sending the survey

**Send survey + photos** hands one file to your phone's normal sharing screen.
Choose whatever works where you are — WhatsApp, email, Google Drive.

If you have no signal, tap **Save to this device instead**. The file waits on
your phone and you can send it later, or copy it off with a cable.

The file contains the answers **and** all the photographs together. You do not
need to send photos separately.

## Things to know

- **Nothing is uploaded automatically.** Data leaves your phone only when you
  send it.
- **Do not clear your browser data** before sending — that deletes surveys.
- **Do not uninstall the app** with unsent surveys on it.
- Photographs are kept at full quality, so a survey can be tens of megabytes.
  Send over wifi where you can.
